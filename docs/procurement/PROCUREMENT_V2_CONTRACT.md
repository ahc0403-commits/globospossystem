# Procurement v2 shared contract (C0)

Status: implementation contract; feature activation is separate from source delivery.

## Authority
POS is the authoritative PR, commercial PO, supplier master and stock store. Office owns review/dispatch operations and accounting projections, invoices, AP/payments and replenishment schedules. Preserve restaurants/restaurant_id and Office store-to-POS mapping. General Office expense purchases are not inventory PRs.

## Envelope
Version 2 JSON; store_id always identifies the POS store at the POS boundary. Office translates scope before calling. Commands carry action, record_id, expected_version, idempotency_key, payload. Only the trusted service-role Office bridge may supply office_actor; POS actors come from auth.uid(). Actor identifiers are namespaced by authority and never coerced into foreign auth.users FKs. Record the actual authenticated principal and approval phase.

## Lifecycle
PR policy 1/2 remains historical. The 2026-10-13 owner decision introduces policy 3: draft -> submitted (Store Manager review/quantity adjustment/approval) -> office_review (buyer final approval) -> approved -> allocated. BM and senior-review transitions are not part of policy 3; price/quantity/comparative-quote validation remains. Requester/store/buyer principals must be distinct, including verified cross-Auth person mappings. Unfinished requests adopt policy 3 with an audit event; issued/approved historical evidence stays unchanged. Returned requests retain history and resubmit a new revision.

PO: immutable issued version -> sent -> confirmed, with receipt progress separate. Existing inventory_purchase_orders.status remains compatible for fulfillment; workflow_version=2 selects the new command contract. V1 commands cannot change v2 documents. Supplier confirmation is required for v2 receiving.
Receiving: accepted quantities alone affect inventory, once, for stock-classified lines. Nonstock/asset receipt and return evidence never creates stock transactions. Classification, conversion and VAT are frozen to the order. Each policy-2 receipt compares against its remaining-quantity snapshot; cumulative accepted quantity cannot exceed the remainder. Corrections/returns are explicit events, never silent confirmed-row edits. An issue resolution must reference an actual confirmed follow-up receipt, actual cancelled remainder, or an Office-validated posted supplier credit adjustment.
Accounting: source order/receipt/line IDs plus immutable revision. Invoice line allocations cannot exceed uninvoiced accepted quantities. A line mismatch blocks AP/payment even if header totals match. Existing invoice-evidence and Finance gates remain authoritative.
Known v2 sources remain required even when their cache is missing. Policy-2 financial hashes cover immutable commercial terms and accepted/returned lines, not channel-payment metadata. A POS nonmonetary status mirror reports invoice/payable/hold/settlement counts and requires review when its version differs or observation is older than ten minutes; it never authorizes payment. Shopee payment evidence enforces caps and holds supplier AP until native Office settlement is reconciled. Native advance clearing and employee-specific reimbursement mapping remain pending; a recorded evidence row is not a payment or journal.
Scheduling: Office creates suggestions, never autonomous purchases/payments. Repeated execution of a store/item/policy/cycle produces one suggestion/request. Missing/stale inventory and unresolved open requests require review.

## Safety and deployment
Capability defaults disabled. Additive DB migrations precede bridge, UI and per-store enablement. Same key + same payload returns original result; same key + different payload fails. Stale versions fail. All commands verify scope and server-owned allowed actions. Preserve stock/payment invariants, legacy ongoing orders and unrelated work. Deploy only through each project's established gates, with exact SHA evidence and explicit distinction between source, applied migration, deployment and operational UAT.
new_requests_enabled pauses new PR creation without disabling in-flight processing. Policy-1 approval/accounting hashes remain compatible after migration. Supplier PO exports use a price-free allowlist; PR exports contain frozen estimates, and internal priced PO exports require price permission. Private export object names include source and file SHA-256 hashes so translated/regenerated outputs do not overwrite prior evidence.

## Bounded reads (2026-10-05)

The default workspace API returns PR/PO summaries (20, maximum 50), catalog pages (200), and one selected detail. Quotes/receipts are paged at 20, issues/returns at 50, and audit events at 100 with keyset cursors. Creation-date filters use Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. Demand, supplier history and legacy repair evidence are explicit opt-ins. Large one-to-many relations are aggregated before joins. Office summary reads batch at most 100 stores/200 orders; snapshots and accounting-status mirrors batch at most 50 POs. Missing or foreign-scope batch results fail without per-row fallbacks. Atomic writes and their exact retry keys remain per document.

## PR-account presentation contract (2026-10-07)

All POS purchasing roles (`inventory_orderer`, `admin`, `store_admin`, `brand_admin`, `super_admin`) open the same paged PR workspace, independent of store policy flags. Missing/disabled policies show the shared disabled workspace and cannot fall back to direct PO creation. Activation and allowed actions remain server-owned. Initial PR entry performs one paged read and does not fetch the legacy item catalog. Receiving, historical POs and explicit PO detail links remain accessible. Successful creation resets list cursors/search/category, uses the recent Vietnam calendar month and selects the returned request ID. Draft/returned edits retain the command journal and optimistic version guard; a server rejection keeps the editor values. Requester deletion is an audited cancellation, with confirmation before the command; it clears the selected detail and refreshes active counts.

The 2026-10-19 owner request aligns current active stores with active ordering-account requirements to the BunsikClub BT policy, including JeonGabok. The migration compares every policy field, audits actual changes once per store, and leaves account identities, access scopes and historical documents unchanged. New-store activation remains explicit. Office principal assignment and native Office access are separate from POS screen parity.

`request_sort=created` opts into `(created_at DESC,id DESC)` and matching `request_before`/`request_before_id` cursors. Omitted sorting keeps the previous updated-at contract for Office callers. `purchase_category` accepts `raw_material`, `tools`, `stationery`, `beverage`, `other`; tools filtering also includes existing stationery rows without rewriting them. `request_group` accepts pending (draft/returned/submitted/brand_review/office_review/senior_review), approved (approved/allocated), cancelled. `request_counts` aggregates all three groups across the store/search/category/creation-date filter, independent of the current page and selected group. `request_view=true` omits unrelated PO summaries and scopes linked POs to the selected PR.

PR PDFs render A4 with formatted Vietnam dates, numeric grouping, server-rounded line amounts and authoritative subtotal/VAT/total, repeated headers, and a grouped approval/total block. Unknown estimates display quote/calculation pending. Supplier PO output retains its price-free projection. ## Native recognition and payment evidence
Nonstock invoice lines require an actual active postable expense account. Asset/CCDC lines require a same-store native registration and acquisition clearing account; invoice recognition and subsequent native capitalization must not debit asset cost twice. Native input-VAT posting retains canonical scope/document enrichment.

Channel payments are evidence records, not money execution. Employee payments bind an actual Office workforce employee and original advance. Office reconciliation consumes only posted native journals, signed cash-voucher lineage or actual advance-settlement receipts, validates store/employee/accounts/document/remaining amounts, and refreshes the canonical payable from real supplier offsets plus native payments. Employee reimbursement does not pay supplier AP again. Pre-invoice company cash/refunds do not manufacture AP. Reversals restore payable balances. Exact logical retries recover the original proof; mismatched amounts fail.

Role roster supports `shared_role` (no HR person) and `person` (verified HR person). Shared assignments require active native Auth identities, store scope, and stage capabilities; caller-supplied names/employee confirmations are discarded. Shared IDs preserve account-level self/distinct approval checks but cannot prove individual human separation. Binh Thanh uses shared role IDs and no deputies. Financial employee payment ownership still requires the actual workforce employee. The roster does not grant/revoke native Auth permissions. Store verifiers use the native fixed-account flow with Super Admin authority, preserving the existing legal-entity verifier. Office buyers use the `purchase_store` template with five purchase actions and no Finance/admin access. Production activation requires created/activated accounts and native authority, followed by operator UAT.

## Raw-material codes, receiving and carryover (2026-10-13)

PR PDFs display the registered product_code below the material name; no procurement QR or menu QR is generated. Code and line-position snapshots do not change historical financial approval hashes. Catalog-only name/code search is capped at 200 and omits PR/PO summaries/counts. Selected products hydrate in one bounded batch.

Policy-3 receivers confirm inspection and post accepted stock in one transaction. Older policies keep independent verifier behavior. Submission, confirmation, stock transactions and immutable supplier-report data are replay-safe. Receipt SQL validates/aggregates sets rather than querying each line. Zero delivery is recorded without stock. Overdelivery cannot silently post beyond the remaining order; rejected excess is reported.

Complete supplier reports require every original ordered line to match cumulative accepted delivery with no current inspection discrepancy. Cancelled or transferred balances never become actual complete delivery. Other receipts generate a discrepancy report including previous/current/cumulative quantities, shortage/excess/rejection, codes, inspector and timestamp, with no prices. PDFs are scoped private objects and transmission is a separate audited command referencing the current file hash, channel and actual delivery evidence.

Shortage queries batch at most 200 source lines. Reservations bind original PO line, PR line and quantity; incompatible material/supplier/quantity, over-reservation and stale balances fail atomically. Cancelled/edited drafts release old reservations. PO issuance transfers the reserved original balance once, retains both PO line IDs and original evidence, and marks the original outcome partial_transferred. The legacy closed status remains compatible for Office; UI and supplier reports distinguish partial transfer from complete receipt. Issuance never posts stock or money.

Selected workflow detail returns the latest 20 receipts, 20 document revisions and 100 approval events; cumulative quantities aggregate all confirmed receipts. Older receipt evidence remains accessible via paged procurement reads. Deployment of the three additive 20261013 migrations and application is separate from source/local checks.
